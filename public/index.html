How it works

Three steps. No friction.

Ritual pairs beautiful physical tiles with a mobile app. Place a tile, tap it daily, and let consistency do the rest.

1

Stick a tile

Place a tile wherever the habit happens — the bathroom mirror, the fridge, the front door.

2

Tap your phone

Hold the back of your phone against the tile — just like tap-and-pay. One tap and the habit is instantly logged.

3

Watch your habits grow

Streaks build momentum. Kids watch their own little tree grow with every habit completed. The whole family tracks progress together.

The science

Notifications are easy to swipe away. Tiles aren't.

Habit research shows that physical cues in specific locations are among the strongest drivers of automatic behaviour. Your brain connects "I'm here" with "I do this."

  • ✓ The tile lives where the habit happens — no phone hunting, no app searching
  • ✓ Location-based triggers build automatic routines faster than reminders
  • ✓ The physical tap creates a micro-moment of commitment — you chose to do it
  • ✓ After a few weeks, the spot itself becomes the reminder
58%
higher success rate

People who linked habits to physical cues and locations had significantly higher success rates than those who relied on app reminders alone.

Based on research into environmental cues and habit formation — Lally et al. (2010), Gardner (2015), Keller et al. (2021)

Questions

Everything you need to know

New to Ritual? Here's what people usually ask.

Ritual tiles are small, beautifully designed physical markers that you place wherever a habit happens — your bathroom mirror, the fridge, a bedside table. For adults, they're premium and minimal. For kids, they're fun 3D icons. Each tile connects to a habit in the app. When you're done with the habit, you tap your phone to the tile and it logs instantly. No batteries, no charging, no Wi‑Fi needed — they work permanently and are completely reusable.
Most habit apps don't work long-term because a button on a screen is easy to ignore. Physical objects are different. Habit research shows that cues placed in specific locations are among the strongest drivers of automatic behaviour — your brain connects "I'm here" with "I do this." Ritual puts the trigger exactly where the habit happens, so after a few weeks the spot itself becomes the reminder. Studies show people who link habits to physical environmental cues have significantly higher success rates than those who rely on app reminders alone.
Unlock your phone, hold the back of it near the tile (close to where your camera is), and hold still for 1–2 seconds. On iPhone a small banner appears at the top — tap it. On Android a notification pops up — tap it. If it doesn't work first time, try shifting your phone slightly up or down — the reader position varies by model.
Everyone in the family gets their own profile. Habits can be individual (each person tracks their own — like homework or taking medication) or shared (one tap counts for everyone — like feeding the pet). Kids earn points and can redeem rewards, but a parent needs to approve and fulfil each reward. Family nudges keep everyone gently accountable without the nagging.
Every habit completion earns points — different habits can be worth different amounts. Adults can redeem rewards instantly. Kids create a request that a parent reviews and fulfils, so rewards stay connected to real life. Points never expire.
Tapped by accident? Just hold your finger on the completed habit card and it undoes the tap. Not near your tile and need to log a habit? You can complete it manually in the app — we make this slightly less convenient on purpose, because the physical tap is the whole point, but life happens.
Yes — tiles are completely reusable. Each tile connects to one habit at a time, but you can reassign it whenever you want. If a habit changes, the tile changes with it.
The tiles are completely passive — no batteries, no power source, no emissions of any kind. They only activate for the moment your phone is held next to them. They're safe to place anywhere in your home. We do recommend securing them to a surface and keeping them out of reach of very young children, as with any small object.
Our story

Built by a real family, for real families

Ritual started at our kitchen table. We're Willem and Christelle — a Melbourne couple with two kids, two full-time jobs, and a household that runs on good intentions but not always great follow-through.

We wanted a way to build better habits as a family — something the kids would actually enjoy, that didn't rely on nagging, and that made the chaos of daily life feel a little more manageable. We couldn't find it, so we built it.

Ritual is our attempt to keep track of the things that matter without taking the fun out of family life. We use it every day. We hope you will too.
