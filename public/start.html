What is TestFlight?

TestFlight is Apple's free app for testing new apps before they launch. After installing TestFlight, you'll be invited to install Ritual — it then appears on your home screen just like any other app. You only need TestFlight once to get started.

How it works

Three steps. No friction.

1

Stick a tile

Place a tile wherever the habit happens — the bathroom mirror, the fridge, the front door.

2

Tap your phone

Hold the back of your phone against the tile — just like tap-and-pay. One tap and the habit is instantly logged.

3

Watch your habits grow

Streaks build momentum. Kids watch their own little tree grow with every habit completed. The whole family tracks progress together.

Your feedback shapes Ritual

As a beta tester, your input is incredibly valuable to us — it directly shapes what Ritual becomes. There are two easy ways to share your thoughts:

📱 Send feedback from the app

While using Ritual, take a screenshot. Then open TestFlight, find Ritual, and tap “Send Beta Feedback”. You can attach your screenshot and write your thoughts — it comes straight to us.

✉️ Email us directly

Prefer to send screenshots or longer messages? Email us anytime at hello@ritualhabits.com.au. We read everything.

Every piece of feedback helps us build a better experience for families like yours. Don't hold back — we want to hear it all.
